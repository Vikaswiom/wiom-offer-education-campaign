How to read this

  • Unique CSPs counts people; Total views counts openings. One person opening a page five times is 1 unique and 5 views. Every figure says which it is.
  • Read rate = unique CSPs who tapped ठीक है ÷ unique CSPs who saw the page. It measures acknowledgement, not comprehension — nobody is asked a question.
  • Rows whose CSP ID did not resolve are counted in views but never as a person. A run of them means a banner is being opened without cspId in the link.
  • Repeat exposure is the one chart counted from raw view volume, because “how often” is the question it asks.
  • Both pages are acknowledgement-only. A read rate below 100% is usually someone closing the WebView with the system back gesture, not disagreement.
